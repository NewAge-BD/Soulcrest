# AION 2 Hidden Cube Spawn Mechanics (숨겨진 큐브 / 히든 큐브 / TW: 隱藏背包 / 隱藏方塊)

Research date: 2026-10-16.

**Method caveat:** The sandbox could not fetch pages directly. WebFetch and curl were blocked by DNS or proxy for destructoid, mein-mmo, allthings.how, aoeah, fextralife and wikily. Every finding below therefore comes from search-engine snippets and summaries of the cited pages, not from reading the full pages. Confidence is graded on that basis. Before quoting any number below as fact, re-check it against the page itself.

Terminology: KR players mostly say "히든 큐브"/"히든큐브" (official PlayNC board titles use "히든 큐브"). Taiwanese and Chinese players often say "隱藏背包" (hidden bag) instead of 隱藏方塊. Search for both terms.

## 1. What is a Hidden Cube and what does it reward?

### Takeaway
A Hidden Cube is a world-placed loot container in the open-world zones, found by sight and by a faint sound. You open it with a 4-second channel, and some cubes need a "Key: Hidden Cube". Key-cubes pay a fixed bundle that includes Soul Crystals, Silentium, Soul Codex, Seed of Detection and Power Shards, plus a small chance at a wing (e.g. Radiant Love Wings). Free cubes exist only in some zones and pay less.

### Cited Findings
- Cubes spawn across the open world. The first player to open one gets the loot, and cubes respawn periodically, so several players can collect them over time — [mein-mmo.de](https://mein-mmo.de/aion-2-cubes-viele-spieler-noch-nie-gesehen-tipps/2/); [gamesfuze](https://gamesfuze.com/game-tips/aion-2-where-to-find-more-hidden-cubes/)
- Opening takes a 4-second channel, and the player who finishes the channel first takes the reward. A monster hit interrupts the channel — [metabot.gg guide](https://metabot.gg/en/aion-2/guides/hidden-cubes-guide)
- Each cube gives off a soft jingling or hum similar to an Empyrean Trace (feather), which helps players locate cubes behind rocks or in water. The sound plays on the Sound Effects channel, so muting SFX also mutes cubes — [aoeah](https://www.aoeah.com/news/4890--aion-2-hidden-cube-locations-map-respawn-time-rewards-sound-keys--farm-route); [raidrelay](https://raidrelay.com/aion-2-hidden-cube-guide)
- Rewards from key-cubes, as listed across guides: Soul Crystals, Silentium, Soul Codex, Seed of Detection, and a Power Shard or Superior Power Shard. One guide says a keyed cube always pays 5 rewards: 1,000 Soul Crystals at 100%, 10–12 Silentium, and a 1% chance of Radiant Love Wings in the wider table — [raidrelay](https://raidrelay.com/aion-2-hidden-cube-guide); [wikily.gg](https://wikily.gg/aion-2/hidden-cubes); [mobalytics](https://mobalytics.gg/gamebase/guides/aion-2-hidden-cubes)
- Free versus key cubes: sources conflict on how to tell them apart. One says gold cubes are rarer, open free, and give slightly fewer rewards than a keyed red cube. Another says yellow cubes open without a key and red cubes need keys. A third says Eltnen and Morheim cubes can be opened free, or with a key for better loot — [games.gg](https://games.gg/aion-2/guides/aion-2-all-hidden-cube-locations/); [gamingpromax](https://gamingpromax.com/aion-2-all-hidden-cube-locations/); [metabot.gg](https://metabot.gg/en/aion-2/hidden-cubes)
- TW guide (game735): cubes "seem interactable without a key, but a key should give better rewards" — [game735](https://www.game735.com/thread-445973-1-1.html)
- Key sources: quests, achievement tiers of "Who Brought the Chest?" (which refunds keys at milestones), Monolith upgrades, daily attendance or login board, the Season Shop and other shops. One guide counts 9 fixed keys per character (quests and achievements) plus 10 per account from the Season Shop. Another says achievements give 34 keys, each once. The key item is "Key: Hidden Cube (Bound)", which is character-bound — [wikily.gg](https://wikily.gg/aion-2/hidden-cubes); [aion2-wiki.wiki](https://www.aion2-wiki.wiki/guide/aion-2-how-to-get-hidden-cube-keys); [kingboost](https://kingboost.net/blogs/aion-2/aion-2-hidden-cubes-guide/)
- KR: keys also came from pre-registration, events and G-Star coupons — [redwendy](https://redwendy.com/archives/2284)
- An Inven tip thread lists what drops from Hidden Cubes (title "히든큐브에서 나오는것들"). Its contents were not retrievable — [Inven 6444/255](https://www.inven.co.kr/board/aion2/6444/255)

### Inferences
- I found no mention that cubes give exploration progress or codex completion directly. The "Soul Codex" listed above is a loot item, not map completion. The only "progress" link found is the key-refund achievement chain "Who Brought the Chest?". Medium confidence.

### Gaps
- No exact drop tables from an official source. The loot rates above come from community data-mining (raidrelay, wikily).
- The colour terms for free and key cubes (gold/yellow vs red) are inconsistent across sources.

## 2. Per-player or shared? Does a cube disappear for others after one player loots it?

### Takeaway
All sources that address this say the cubes are shared: first come, first served, and a cube vanishes once someone opens it. There is no evidence of personal or instanced cubes. Confidence is high that cubes are shared within a channel. The cross-channel behaviour is not confirmed.

### Cited Findings
- Destructoid describes cubes as "chance-based and server-wide… shared among all players within the server". Each time one is opened, a new one appears at a random spot — [Destructoid](https://www.destructoid.com/aion-2-hidden-cube-locations-best-spots-and-how-to-get-keys/)
- First come, first served, and whoever finds a cube first claims the reward — [mein-mmo.de](https://mein-mmo.de/aion-2-cubes-viele-spieler-noch-nie-gesehen-tipps/2/); [gamesfuze](https://gamesfuze.com/game-tips/aion-2-where-to-find-more-hidden-cubes/)
- Map markers show where a cube can appear, not what is currently present on your server. Empty markers are normal — [wikily.gg](https://wikily.gg/aion-2/hidden-cubes); [metabot.gg](https://metabot.gg/en/aion-2/hidden-cubes)
- Guides advise farming in low-population areas or at off-peak times, such as weekday mornings or right after maintenance. That advice only makes sense if cubes are shared — [mein-mmo.de](https://mein-mmo.de/aion-2-cubes-viele-spieler-noch-nie-gesehen-tipps/2/)
- Bahamut (TW): a bag opened by someone else has to respawn before it reappears — [Bahamut snA=988](https://forum.gamer.com.tw/C.php?bsn=82913&snA=988)
- KR: as competition grew, switching channels and running circular routes across several channels became the standard method — [pandarank](https://pandarank.net/contents/69268bc72b26afcbc18e8c6d)

### Inferences
- The channel-hopping meta strongly implies that cube state is held per channel: each channel has its own live cube instances. If cubes were server-wide across channels, channel switching would not help. Medium confidence. No source states this explicitly.
- Destructoid's phrase "server-wide" most likely means "shared, not personal", not "one state across all channels". This reading is my own; treat it with low to medium confidence.

### Gaps
- No official statement on whether cubes are per player, per channel or per server. No source tested whether a cube opened in channel 1 is still present in channel 2.

## 3. Respawn timer: fixed, random or reset-based?

### Takeaway
There is no official or confirmed timer. The most consistent observation is that the timer is variable, not fixed. Community estimates range from minutes up to about 3 hours. A data-mined value suggests a random delay of 1 to 60 minutes. Several sources say the trigger is the looting itself: opening a cube spawns a replacement at a different candidate position.

### Cited Findings
- KR blog: respawn time is not officially published. Its own tests show cubes generally reappear within 30 minutes to 3 hours, sometimes within a few minutes if you are lucky — [redwendy](https://redwendy.com/archives/2284)
- Official PlayNC KR user tip board, part 2: the respawn time "doesn't seem to be exactly fixed", so check spots whenever you have time. This is a user post on the official forum, not a developer statement — [PlayNC tip 2](https://aion2.plaync.com/ko-kr/board/qnanknowhow/view?articleId=69423fbd1294f75cb5c4ec64); [PlayNC tip 1](https://aion2.plaync.com/ko-kr/board/qnanknowhow/view?articleId=692ff1c127be63556e2a38a8)
- Raidrelay reads a delay of 1 minute to 1 hour in the client files and labels this its own interpretation, possibly the respawn delay. It says no respawn time has been confirmed — [raidrelay](https://raidrelay.com/aion-2-hidden-cube-guide)
- aoeah and expcarry: there is no verified global respawn timer in the client data, and a "30-minute respawn" is a community guess. Do not quote it as a Global rule — [aoeah](https://www.aoeah.com/news/4890--aion-2-hidden-cube-locations-map-respawn-time-rewards-sound-keys--farm-route); [expcarry](https://expcarry.com/aion-2-hidden-cubes-guide)
- allthings.how and himeb cite Korean reports of about 30 minutes to 3 hours, unconfirmed for Global — [allthings.how](https://allthings.how/aion-2-hidden-cubes-how-to-find-and-open-them/); [himeb](https://himeb.com/en/aion2-hidden-cube-guide/)
- Trigger: aoeah describes the system as a gathering-node pool. A cube disappears when opened and another appears elsewhere, usually not nearby. Destructoid and wikily also say a new cube appears at a random spot whenever one is opened — [aoeah](https://www.aoeah.com/news/4890--aion-2-hidden-cube-locations-map-respawn-time-rewards-sound-keys--farm-route); [Destructoid](https://www.destructoid.com/aion-2-hidden-cube-locations/); [wikily.gg](https://wikily.gg/aion-2/hidden-cubes)
- Conflicting claims: Baidu Zhidao says about 1 hour. Bahamut players say the time is unknown. A biubiu accelerator article claims a daily refresh at random positions without evidence, so it is low quality — [Baidu Zhidao](https://zhidao.baidu.com/question/1682546388714037387.html); [Bahamut snA=988](https://forum.gamer.com.tw/C.php?bsn=82913&snA=988); [biubiu](https://www.biubiu001.com/news/172562.html)

### Inferences
- Best-supported model (medium confidence): opening a cube triggers despawn. After a random delay, likely 1–60 minutes per the data-mine or longer according to observers, the group spawns a replacement at a random candidate spot. The longer 30-minute to 3-hour figures observed in KR likely mix the random delay with the chance that the replacement lands at a spot the observer was not watching.
- I found no evidence of a server-reset or daily-reset based respawn. The single "daily refresh" claim is unreliable.

### Gaps
- No official NCSoft value. Nobody has run a systematic, timestamped measurement with many samples.

## 4. Candidate-position pools and how many cubes are active at once

### Takeaway
Cubes are organised as spawn groups. Each group has one live cube that appears at one of many candidate spots. The global client data-mine most commonly cited lists 206 groups and 3,722 candidate spots across 4 zones. That means 206 live cubes at most per channel, and only about 5.5% of map markers are occupied at any time. Confidence is medium to high for the structure. The exact counts vary by source and version.

### Cited Findings
- 206 cube groups choose among 3,722 possible spots: Verteron 95 groups / 1,440 spots; Eltnen 26 / 456; Altgard 59 / 1,371; Morheim 26 / 455 — [wikily.gg](https://wikily.gg/aion-2/hidden-cubes); [metabot.gg](https://metabot.gg/en/aion-2/hidden-cubes); [space4games](https://space4games.com/en/games-en/aion-2-hidden-cubes/)
- A returning cube can appear at any spot in its group, not necessarily where you found it, so camping a single spot is discouraged — [metabot.gg](https://metabot.gg/en/aion-2/hidden-cubes)
- Sub-zone examples from metabot's tables: Altamia Canyon has 9 cubes and 90 possible spots; Aullaeu Village has 6 cubes and 95 possible spots — [metabot.gg](https://metabot.gg/en/aion-2/hidden-cubes)
- Conflicting counts:
  - gamers4.life (JP) lists 2,772 positions: Verteron 1,400, Altgard 1,371, Reshanta 1 — [gamers4.life](https://gamers4.life/aion-2/database/ja/hidden-cubes/)
  - aion2.blog lists 172 cubes: Verteron 102, Altgard 69, Reshanta 1 — [aion2.blog](https://aion2.blog/hidden-cubes/)
  - game735 (TW) lists 71 Elyos (Verteron incl. islands) and 52 Asmodian (Altgard) — [game735](https://www.game735.com/thread-445973-1-1.html)
  - Bahamut map shows 52 bag spots, but a player complains the markers are badly misplaced — [Bahamut snA=988](https://forum.gamer.com.tw/C.php?bsn=82913&snA=988); [Bahamut map thread snA=1574](https://forum.gamer.com.tw/C.php?bsn=82913&snA=1574)
- Bahamut: a fixed spot doesn't necessarily respawn, and points may rotate among several candidate spots — [Bahamut snA=988](https://forum.gamer.com.tw/C.php?bsn=82913&snA=988)
- KR fmkorea user mapped the positions of 250 opened Elyos cubes — [fmkorea](https://www.fmkorea.com/9691894464)
- One KR guide reads as if positions are fixed and first-arriver-takes. That partially conflicts with the random-pool model, but it fits a "fixed candidate set" reading — [PlayNC tip 1](https://aion2.plaync.com/ko-kr/board/qnanknowhow/view?articleId=692ff1c127be63556e2a38a8)

### Inferences
- The low counts from early KR/TW maps (52–172) probably count observed live cubes or groups. The high counts (1,371–3,722) count candidate positions. These are different metrics, not real contradictions. Medium confidence.
- With about 18 candidates per group on average (3,722 / 206), a looted spot will rarely refill at the same place.

### Gaps
- No source confirms whether the 206/3,722 figures are per channel. Eltnen and Morheim data may be KR/TW-only (see section 5).

## 5. Patch history (KR/TW/global) and dates

### Takeaway
KR/TW launched on 2025-11-19. A December 2025 patch series added key-only cubes, fixed achievement credit and the key stack cap, and changed wing drops. The developers said they were considering more cube spawns because players had a key surplus. Global's spawn data shows Verteron and Altgard as entirely key-only.

### Cited Findings
- 2025-12-02 KR live stream: asked about too many leftover keys, the developers said they would consider expanding cube spawns ("큐브 리젠 확대") and adding more uses for keys — [Inven news 311790](https://www.inven.co.kr/webzine/news/?news=311790&site=aion2); [Ruliweb summary](https://m.ruliweb.com/pc/board/300007/read/2337946)
- 2025-12-03 KR update: summary reports that the patch note reverted the wing drop rate from Hidden Cubes to the previous state. This claim comes from a search summary and was not verified against the note text — [Ruliweb 12/3 note](https://bbs.ruliweb.com/pc/board/300007/read/2337987); [NCSoft 12/03 update news](https://about.ncsoft.com/news/article/aion2_update_251203)
- 2025-12-10 KR (fextralife lists it as 2025-12-09 TW/KR): added "key-only Hidden Cubes" throughout Atreia — [Inven 12/10 patch note](https://www.inven.co.kr/board/aion2/6388/39438); [fextralife Patch Notes](https://aion2.wiki.fextralife.com/Patch_Notes)
- 2025-12-17 KR: opening cubes that need achievement keys now credits the related achievement, and the max stack of "Hidden Cube Key" was raised — [Inven 12/17 note](https://www.inven.co.kr/board/aion2/6388/45636)
- 2025-12-24 KR: Hidden Cubes from Rift of Space-Time (시공의 균열) now drop wings matching your own race — [Inven 12/24 note](https://www.inven.co.kr/board/aion2/6388/51503); [Ruliweb 12/24](https://bbs.ruliweb.com/pc/board/300007/read/2339292)
- Global: every cube in Verteron and Altgard uses the key-only object, with the prompt "Cannot open without a key". Eltnen and Morheim cubes can spawn as regular free cubes — [metabot.gg](https://metabot.gg/en/aion-2/hidden-cubes); [wikily.gg](https://wikily.gg/aion-2/hidden-cubes)
- Conflict: one guide says Eltnen and Morheim (Chapter 1, levels 45–50) are live in KR/TW but not on Global as of October 2026, while other guides describe them as present in Global client data — [metabot.gg guide](https://metabot.gg/en/aion-2/guides/hidden-cubes-guide); [wikily.gg](https://wikily.gg/aion-2/hidden-cubes)
- The October 2026 global patch notes on fextralife do not mention cubes — [fextralife Patch Notes](https://aion2.wiki.fextralife.com/Patch_Notes)

### Inferences
- Whether the "expand cube regen" consideration from December 2025 was ever implemented is not documented in what I found.

### Gaps
- No 2026 KR/TW patch notes about cube spawn changes were found.
- Exact global launch date and global-specific cube changes were not confirmed in this pass.

## 6. Summary of conflicts and confidence

### Takeaway
Several facts are solid: cubes are shared and first-come, opening takes a 4-second channel, there is no official timer, and spawns rotate within candidate pools. Respawn duration, free-cube colours, zone counts and the global availability of Eltnen and Morheim are all contested.

### Cited Findings
- Respawn: 1–60 minutes data-mined ([raidrelay](https://raidrelay.com/aion-2-hidden-cube-guide)), about 1 hour ([Baidu](https://zhidao.baidu.com/question/1682546388714037387.html)), 30 minutes to 3 hours observed ([redwendy](https://redwendy.com/archives/2284)), "not fixed" ([PlayNC tip 2](https://aion2.plaync.com/ko-kr/board/qnanknowhow/view?articleId=69423fbd1294f75cb5c4ec64)), "30 minutes is a guess" ([aoeah](https://www.aoeah.com/news/4890--aion-2-hidden-cube-locations-map-respawn-time-rewards-sound-keys--farm-route)). Confidence that the timer is variable: high. Confidence in any specific number: low.
- Position model: random within a group ([metabot.gg](https://metabot.gg/en/aion-2/hidden-cubes)) versus a fixed-spot reading ([PlayNC tip 1](https://aion2.plaync.com/ko-kr/board/qnanknowhow/view?articleId=692ff1c127be63556e2a38a8)). The pool model is better supported: medium to high confidence.
- Destructoid calls cubes "ultra rare" and server-wide ([Destructoid](https://www.destructoid.com/aion-2-hidden-cube-locations/)). "Ultra rare" fits poorly with 206 concurrent groups per channel, so it is likely hyperbole.

### Inferences
- For game-design reference: the AION 2 pattern is a shared, per-channel, loot-triggered despawn, followed by a random delay and a respawn at a random candidate spot within a fixed group pool. Some groups require keys.

### Gaps
- There is no official NCSoft documentation of the cube spawn rules. All mechanics come from community observation or data-mining.
